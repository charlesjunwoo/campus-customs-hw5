2 · Three new problems this team and these tools could solve

#ProblemHow the current team handles itProof from this run
1An order where a size is out of stock and a vendor restock is required Inventory checks the SKU and size, identifies the issue and which vendor can restock it (and whether that vendor is available), then passes the result to Accounting for any needed payment or approval. Ticket 101 (tee S, shortfall 1 → $8.00 PO) and 103 (hoodie M, shortfall 12 → $264.00 PO): check_inventory → find_restock_vendor → submit_purchase_order
2An order where Accounting has to appeal to a human for approval Accounting prepares the payment, purchase order, or price quote with the amount computed from the database, and it waits as pending_human in "Needs you" until I approve or reject it. Five requests went to me; I approved four and rejected one. The agents approved nothing.
3An order that needs Customer Service directly If the operational work is already understood but the customer needs an update, Customer Service reads the ticket and the board and drafts the reply without re-running the whole team. Drafts to Tauhid Zaman (101) and Yale AI Club (103) were based on what was already on the board, with no internal details and no promised dates.

3 · Three problems it could not solve

#ProblemWhy notWhat it would take
1Employee shifts or payroll We don't have employee, scheduling, timesheet, or payroll data, so the current agents and MCP tools have nothing to work with. New tables (employees, shifts, timesheets, pay rates), MCP tools over them, and a staffing agent. Payroll is money out, so it would go through the same human approval route.
2Financial forecasting Accounting can inspect current cash, invoices, rent, pricing, and purchase requests, but it can't really forecast the future, because there is no historical sales or revenue data, which we would need at a minimum. Cash only goes out in this model; the Cash tab is a snapshot, not a trend. Sales and revenue history (and inflows to cash), then a forecasting tool Accounting can call.
3Real communications beyond drafts Customer Service and Facilities can prepare drafts, but the system does not presently send real emails or messages. Every draft on the board is sent = 0 by design. To do that safely, we would need controlled communication tools and maybe another human approval step: an "approve and send" for drafts, just like payments.

4 · What would have been simpler?

A single agent with the right MCP tools could probably have handled a lot of the basic tasks, like checking inventory, invoices, pricing, and cash. But having a Boss that is smart and able to run a team of agents with the structure we set up was more efficient for token use across very different tasks, because it separated operational inventory checking, accounting, communications, and human interfacing for approval.
